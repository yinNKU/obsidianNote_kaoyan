【例 24】（2006，数二）求极限 $\lim_{x\to0}\frac{1}{x^{3}}\left[\left(\frac{2+\cos x}{3}\right)^{x}-1\right]$ .

  

解【方法1】 原式 $= \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{1}{x^3} [\mathrm{e}^{x\ln \left(\frac{2 + \cos x}{3}\right)} - 1]$

  

$$

= \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {x \ln \left(\frac {2 + \cos x}{3}\right)}{x ^ {3}} \quad (\text {等价无穷小代换})

$$

  

$$

= \lim _ {x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\ln \left(1 + \frac {\cos x - 1}{3}\right)}{x ^ {2}}

$$

  

$$

= \lim _ {x \to 0} {\frac {\cos x - 1}{3 x ^ {2}}} \quad (\text {等价无穷小代换})

$$

  

$$

= \lim _ {x \rightarrow 0} \frac {- \frac {1}{2} x ^ {2}}{3 x ^ {2}} = - \frac {1}{6}.

$$

$$

$$

  

$$

= \lim _ {x \rightarrow 0} \frac {\cos x - 1}{3 x ^ {2}}

$$

  

$$

= \lim _ {x \to 0} \frac {- \frac {1}{2} x ^ {2}}{3 x ^ {2}} = - \frac {1}{6}.

$$

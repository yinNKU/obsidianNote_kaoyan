# 学习册p159/例10

## 题目
> 【例10】（2005，数二、三）计算二重积分 $\iint_{D}|x^{2} + y^{2} - 1|\mathrm{d}\sigma$ ，其中
>
>
>
> $$
>
> D = \{(x, y) \mid 0 \leqslant x \leqslant 1, 0 \leqslant y \leqslant 1 \}.
>
> $$
>
>
> 如图 7 所示, 将 D 分成 $D_{1}$ 与 $D_{2}$ 两部分.
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \begin{array}{l} \iint_ {D} \mid x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 1 \mid \mathrm{d} \sigma \\ = \iint_ {D _ {1}} (1 - x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} \sigma + \iint_ {D _ {2}} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 1) \mathrm{d} \sigma \\ = \iint_ {D _ {1}} (1 - x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} \sigma + \left[ \iint_ {D} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 1) \mathrm{d} \sigma - \iint_ {D _ {1}} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 1) \mathrm{d} \sigma \right] \\ = 2 \iint_ {D _ {1}} (1 - x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} \sigma + \iint_ {D} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 1) \mathrm{d} \sigma \\ \end{array}
>
> $$

## 正确答案
> 由于
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \iint_ {D _ {1}} (1 - x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} \sigma = \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \mathrm{d} \theta \int_ {0} ^ {1} (1 - r ^ {2}) r \mathrm{d} r = \frac {\pi}{8},
>
> $$
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \iint_ {D} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 1) \mathrm{d} \sigma = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {0} ^ {1} (x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} - 1) \mathrm{d} y = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \left(x ^ {2} - \frac {2}{3}\right) \mathrm{d} x = - \frac {1}{3},
>
> $$
>
>
>
> 因此 $\iint_{D}|x^{2} + y^{2} - 1|\mathrm{d}\sigma = \frac{\pi}{4} -\frac{1}{3}.$

- 积分区域的可加性

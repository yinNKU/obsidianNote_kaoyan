# 学习册p80/例7/4

## 题目
> 求不定积分$\int e^{x}\sin^{2}xdx.$
> $$
>
> (4) \int \mathrm{e} ^ {x} \sin^ {2} x \mathrm{d} x = \int \mathrm{e} ^ {x} \cdot \frac {1}{2} (1 - \cos 2 x) \mathrm{d} x = \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{e} ^ {x} - \frac {1}{2} \int \mathrm{e} ^ {x} \cos 2 x \mathrm{d} x,
>
> $$
>
>
>
> 而 $\int \mathrm{e}^x\cos 2x\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{5}\mathrm{e}^x\cos 2x + \frac{2}{5}\mathrm{e}^x\sin 2x + C,$
>
>
>
> 故 $\int \mathrm{e}^x\sin^2 x\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}^x -\frac{1}{10}\mathrm{e}^x\cos 2x - \frac{1}{5}\mathrm{e}^x\sin 2x + C.$

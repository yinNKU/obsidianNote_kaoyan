# 学习册p199/例4

## 题目
> 【例 4】（2004）设 L 为正向圆周 $x^{2} + y^{2} = 2$ 在第一象限中的部分，则曲线积分 $\int_{L} x \, dy - 2y \, dx$ 的值为 ____.

## 正确答案
> 解【方法1】令 $x = \sqrt{2}\cos t, y = \sqrt{2}\sin t$ ，则
>
>
>
> 原式 $= \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\bigl[(\sqrt{2}\cos t)^{2} + 2(\sqrt{2}\sin t)^{2}\bigr]\mathrm{d}t = \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(2\cos^{2}t + 4\sin^{2}t)\mathrm{d}t$
>
>
>
> $$
>
> = 6 \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \sin^ {2} t \mathrm{d} t = 6 \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {\pi}{2} = \frac {3}{2} \pi .
>
> $$
>
>
>
> 【方法 2】 补线用格林公式.
>
>
>
> 补上线段 $L_{1}: (0,0) \to (\sqrt{2},0), L_{2}: (0,\sqrt{2}) \to (0,0)$ . 设 $D = \{(x,y) \mid x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0, x^{2} + y^{2} \leqslant 2\}$ , 则有
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \int_ {L} x \mathrm{d} y - 2 y \mathrm{d} x = \oint_ {L + L _ {1} + L _ {2}} x \mathrm{d} y - 2 y \mathrm{d} x - \int_ {L _ {1}} x \mathrm{d} y - 2 y \mathrm{d} x - \int_ {L _ {2}} x \mathrm{d} y - 2 y \mathrm{d} x
>
> $$
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \begin{array}{l} = \iint_ {D} 3 \mathrm{d} x \mathrm{d} y - \int_ {0} ^ {\sqrt {2}} 0 \mathrm{d} x - \int_ {\sqrt {2}} ^ {0} 0 \mathrm{d} y \\ = 3 S _ {D} = 3 \times \frac {2 \pi}{4} = \frac {3}{2} \pi . \\ \end{array}
>
> $$

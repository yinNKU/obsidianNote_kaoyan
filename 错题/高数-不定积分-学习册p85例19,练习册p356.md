# 学习册p85/例19，练习册p35/6

## 题目
> 【例 19】设 $f(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\mathrm{e}^{x},&x\geqslant0,\\&\cos x,&x<0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 则 $\int f(x)\mathrm{d}x=$ ____.

## 正确答案
> 解【方法1】 $\int f(x)\mathrm{d}x = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\mathrm{e}^x +C_1, & x\geqslant 0,\\ \sin x + C_2, & x <   0. \end{array} \right.$ 由于原函数必连续，又
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {+}} (e ^ {x} + C _ {1}) = 1 + C _ {1}, \lim _ {x \to 0 ^ {-}} (\sin x + C _ {2}) = C _ {2},
>
> $$
>
>
>
> 则 $1 + C_{1} = C_{2}$ .令 $C_1 = C$ ，则 $C_2 = 1 + C$
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \int f (x) \mathrm{d} x = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \mathrm{e} ^ {x} + C, & x \geqslant 0, \\ \sin x + 1 + C, & x <   0. \end{array} \right.
>
> $$
>
>
>
> 【方法2】由于 $f(x) = \begin{cases} e^x, & x \geqslant 0, \\ \cos x, & x < 0 \end{cases}$ 是一个连续函数，则 $F(x) = \int_{0}^{x} f(t) \, \mathrm{d}t$ 为 $f(x)$ 的一个原函数， $\int f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x = F(x) + C.$
>
>
>
> $$
>
> \begin{array}{l} F (x) = \int_ {0} ^ {x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \int_ {0} ^ {x} \mathrm{e} ^ {t} \mathrm{d} t, & x \geqslant 0 \\ \int_ {0} ^ {x} \cos t \mathrm{d} t, & x <   0 \end{array} \right. \\ = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \mathrm{e} ^ {x} - 1, & x \geqslant 0, \\ \sin x, & x <   0. \end{array} \right. \\ \end{array}
>
> $$
>
>  求不定积分 $\int \max \{1, |x|\} \mathrm{d}x$ .
>  答案：$$
>
> 6. \left\{ \begin{array}{c c} {- \frac {1}{2} x ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} + C,} & {x <   - 1,} \\ {x + C,} & {- 1 \leqslant x \leqslant 1,} \\ {\frac {1}{2} x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} + C,} & {x > 1} \end{array} \right.
>
> $$

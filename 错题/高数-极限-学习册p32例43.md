# 学习册p32/例43

## 题目
> 【例 43】设 $x_{1}>0, x_{n+1}=\frac{1}{2}\left(x_{n}+\frac{1}{x_{n}}\right), n=1,2,\cdots$ . 求极限 $\lim_{n\to\infty}x_{n}$ .

## 正确答案
> 由题设知 $x_{n} > 0$ ，且
>
>
>
> $$
>
> x _ {n + 1} = \frac {1}{2} \Big (x _ {n} + \frac {1}{x _ {n}} \Big) = \frac {1}{2} \Big [ (\sqrt {x _ {n}}) ^ {2} + \Big (\frac {1}{\sqrt {x _ {n}}} \Big) ^ {2} \Big ] \geqslant \frac {1}{2} \cdot 2 \sqrt {x _ {n}} \cdot \frac {1}{\sqrt {x _ {n}}} = 1,
>
> $$
>
>
>
> $$
>
> x _ {n + 1} - x _ {n} = \frac {1}{2} \Big (\frac {1}{x _ {n}} - x _ {n} \Big) = \frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {1 - x _ {n} ^ {2}}{x _ {n}} \leqslant 0, \qquad (n \geqslant 2)
>
> $$
>
>
>
> 或 $\frac{x_{n + 1}}{x_n} = \frac{1}{2}\Big(1 + \frac{1}{x_n^2}\Big)\leqslant \frac{1}{2}\Big(1 + \frac{1}{1}\Big) = 1,$ $n\geqslant 2$
>
>
>
> 则数列 $\{x_{n}\}$ 单调递减且下有界，极限 $\lim_{n\to \infty}x_n$ 存在，设 $\lim_{n\to \infty}x_n = a.$
>
>
>
> 等式 $x_{n + 1} = \frac{1}{2}\left(x_n + \frac{1}{x_n}\right)$ 两端取极限得
>
>
>
> $$
>
> a = \frac {1}{2} \left(a + \frac {1}{a}\right),
>
> $$
>
>
>
> 得 $a^2 = 1$ ，则 $a = 1, a = -1$ （舍去），故 $\lim_{n\to \infty}x_n = 1.$

## 错因
- 对递推公式两边同时求极限

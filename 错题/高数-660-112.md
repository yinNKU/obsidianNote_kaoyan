## 题目
交换积分次序

$$
\int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} d\theta \int_{0}^{2\cos\theta} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) r dr = \underline{\quad\quad\quad}
$$

## 正确答案


$$
\iint_D f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, r \, d\theta dr = 
\int_0^{\sqrt{2}} r \, dr \int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\arccos\frac{r}{2}} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, d\theta 
+ 
\int_{\sqrt{2}}^{2} r \, dr \int_{-\arccos\frac{r}{2}}^{\arccos\frac{r}{2}} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, d\theta
$$
